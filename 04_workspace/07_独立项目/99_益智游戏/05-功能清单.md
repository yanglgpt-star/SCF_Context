## 七、数据库 DDL

> 用于在云开发控制台创建集合及索引

### users 集合
- 索引：`openId`（唯一）
- 字段：openId, role, nickName, stars, currentStars, level, treeGrowth, badges, streak, createdAt

### tasks 集合
- 索引：`status`
- 字段：title, category, difficulty, description, tips, verifyType, voiceUrl, dangerTips, status, createdAt

### task_logs 集合
- 索引：`userId` + `assignedAt`（联合）
- 字段：userId, taskId, assignedAt, completedAt, status, verifyMaterial, photoUrl, audioUrl, rewardStars, reviewedAt

### wishes 集合
- 索引：`userId`
- 字段：userId, title, icon, costStars, status, redeemedAt, createdAt
